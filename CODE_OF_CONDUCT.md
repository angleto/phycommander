# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

In short: **be welcoming, be patient, be direct about technical disagreements, never make it personal.**

## Summary

- Engage around the work, not around people.
- Unacceptable: harassment, doxxing, discriminatory jokes, sustained personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, or otherwise making the project an uncomfortable place to contribute.
- The maintainer reserves the right to delete, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to ban (temporarily or permanently) any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

Report abuse by emailing **angelo@leto.blue** with subject `[PhyCMD CoC]`. Reports are confidential. Expect acknowledgement within 7 days.

The full Contributor Covenant text is authoritative; this page is a pointer.
